# Version history

*[Version française](historique-versions.md)*

Every version that changes what the country measures, publishes or narrates is recorded here, with its delivery date (installation usually follows within 24 hours). A day's data should always be read with the firmware version stated in the file.

| Delivered | Firmware | Update | What changes for the data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 Oct 2026 | 0.3 | Production start | Era 1 begins. The newspaper appears at 8 pm. No raw export yet. |
| 10 Oct 2026 | 0.4 | Update 1a | The newspaper tells what changed since the day before (fewer repetitions), rotating section, "Figure of the day". Cable-free maintenance. Save format unchanged. |
| 11 Oct 2026 | 0.5 | Update 1b-1 "Small stories" | The newspaper follows characters (surviving ministers, "Whatever happened to…?", candidates, firsts). Embassy denials. Save format unchanged. |
| 13 Oct 2026 (installation planned 14 Oct, 9 pm) | 0.6 | Update 1b-2 "The newsroom" | **Daily raw export** to this repository (format 1), SHA-256 manifest, system notes. Citizen journalists, sections, humour column, Sunday edition, censored press (articles only, never the figures). Website published in a single commit. Fixes: "d'électricité" elision, tie-break by anger, no "satisfied mood" brief, 4th denial. Country save format unchanged (3440 bytes). First raw file expected on 15 October; it starts at installation (`heuresManquantes` = `null`). |
| 18 Oct 2026 (installation 9 pm) | 0.7 | Update 1b-3, "seeds and plug" step | Raw file **format 2**: `graines` block (era seed, gateway and region seeds, the day's new seeds with time and board); **hourly Shelly plug reading** (`heures[].prise`: power and energy counter of the whole country, read locally); gateway **Wi-Fi reconnection** every 5 min after an outage, recorded in `systeme.notes` (type `wifi`). Radio protocol version 4 (a region's hello message carries its two seeds, 25 bytes): all 7 boards are updated together; a board on another version is rejected and reported (`version` note). Country, newspaper and country save format unchanged (3440 bytes). First format 2 file expected on 19 October. |
| 24 Oct 2026 (installed 25 Oct, 9 pm) | 0.8 | Update 1b-3, continued "Elections" | **Format 3** of the raw file: each hour's speaker (`heures[].porteVoix`), each region's measurements (`heures[].regions[].systeme`: memory, computation time, reply time, temperature) and the gateway's temperature, **weights of all 120 brains** and **complete lexicons** once a day, vote detail (`election`), poll (`sondage`), fame, `depouilleTick` for each idea. Electoral features open one per evening from 26 October: abstention and the election column (26th), blank vote (27th), polls (28th), fame (the day after the Age of Memory); ministers' names in the issue. Newspaper corrections: "Your regions" under censorship, "(yesterday)", the time of each word, "The vote is tonight", no duplicate market brief, signatory of the newsroom introduction. Radio protocol version 5 (all 7 boards together); country save in **format 4** (11,424 bytes), converted without loss from format 3 (`migration` note); region saves unchanged. First format 3 file expected on 26 October. |

Days from 9 to 14 October 2026 are available in rebuilt form (`donnees/ere-001/reconstitue/`).
